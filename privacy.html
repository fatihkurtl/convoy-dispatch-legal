Privacy Policy – Convoy Dispatch

Effective date: 27 November 2026

Convoy Dispatch (“the game”) is an offline puzzle game for mobile devices made by FKT Yazılım (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information is handled when you play the game, by whom, and the choices you have.

In short: the game has no accounts and we run no servers for it. We do not collect, receive or sell your personal information ourselves. Your progress stays on your device. Information is handled by Google for ads, store purchases and (only if you choose it) cloud saving, as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (cleared levels, stars, coins, unlocked items), your settings (language, sound, music, vibration, reminders) and your purchases' unlocks in a file on your device. This file is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: an occasional full-screen ad between levels, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for example for a hint). To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and how you interact with ads.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's settings.
  • The “Remove ads” purchase turns off the full-screen ads between levels.

How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are made through Google Play (or the Apple App Store on iOS devices). Payment details are handled entirely by the store; we never see them. The game only receives a confirmation from the store on your device so it can unlock what you bought.

4. Optional cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, the game may sign you in to it when it starts; your Play Games profile is managed by Google. Only if you tap Save your progress in the settings does the game store a copy of your progress file in your Google account's saved games, so you can restore it on another device. We have no access to your Google account. You can delete this saved data in the Google Play Games settings.

5. Reminders

The game can show reminders (for example when a new daily yard is ready). They are scheduled on your device; no server is involved. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in your device's notification settings.

6. Game engine diagnostics

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity may receive basic technical information such as the device model, operating system and engine errors, to keep the engine working. See Unity's privacy policy.

7. Link to Convoy Tycoon

The game includes an optional link to our other game, Convoy Tycoon. Tapping it opens the app store page. The link carries only a campaign label telling the store that the visit came from this game; no personal information is added to it.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your rights

Because we do not hold your personal information, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You can delete your data at any time by uninstalling the game, deleting the cloud copy in Google Play Games, and resetting your advertising ID. For data handled by Google, see Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK) you have further rights; contact us with any question.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

11. Contact
